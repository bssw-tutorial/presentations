Write a  routine “createMesh”  to create a cartesian mesh

boundaries[NDIM] - boundary conditions along each dimension


Its outputs are:
float domainbnds[NDIM][HIGH] coordinates of the lower left corner and

int domainbc[NDIM] boundary conditions along each dimension


data structure for a list of blocks

int blkID – unique identity of the block, blocks should be numbered  in lexicographic order
float bbnd[NDIM][HIGH] – coordinates of the lower left corner and upper right corner

write a routine move_particles that physically moves particles in the domain by
X number of steps, X being specified by the user. For each step, each
particle changes its position by -1.5 along each dimension. After each
step check for each particle if it is in the same block or not. If it is not
find its new block and update the particle. If the returned value of new block is -1, then the particle has to handle boundary conditions by calling another routine handle_boundary. In this routine the following actions are taken. At first we will assume that only periodic boundary conditions are applied. Particle may not be outside the boundary for every axis. 
For every dimension of the particle check if it is outside the boundary. If for  any dimension it is still within the boundary do not change the coordinate along that dimension. For PERIODIC boundary conditions its new position is L + pos[d] if it outside on the LOW side and L-pos[d] if it outside on the HIGH side where L  = domainbnds[d][HIGH] - domainbnds[d][LOW]
After all the dimensions are processed and new positions are determined along each dimension update the associated blockID.

For any dimension where the particle is outside the boundary and the boundary condition is REFLECTIVE its new position is 2*domainbnds[d][e] - pos[d] where d is the dimension, and e is LOW or HIGH depending on which side of the domain the particle is outside.
